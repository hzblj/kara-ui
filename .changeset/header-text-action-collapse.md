A header `text` action takes `collapsedIcon` and `collapsed`, for a label that makes room for a title revealed on scroll. On iOS 26 a trailing action with a `testID` morphs: the label fades, its glass stretches a little and springs into a circle, and the icon scales in; a press still swells the glass. Elsewhere the label and the icon swap. The morph is the new `NavigationItemMorph` in kara-native.

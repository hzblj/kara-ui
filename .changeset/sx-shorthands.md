Layout shorthands on the React Native `sx`: `row`, `center`, `fill` (`flex: 1`), `absolute` (out of the flow and over the parent, all four edges at 0) and `size` (width and height at once), and `wrap`, `grow` and `shrink` take `true`. Each gives way to the key it stands for, so `flexDirection`, `align`, `flex`, `position`, a side of its own or `w` and `h` beside one win. All of them take a value per breakpoint.

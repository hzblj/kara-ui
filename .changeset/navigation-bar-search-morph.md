`NavigationBar`'s integrated search (`placement: 'integratedButton'`) opens the way the system's own does on iOS 26: the search button grows into the field with the bar's Liquid Glass morph and the cancel splits off its end, then merges back and the field shrinks into the button on close. `search.buttonPlacement: 'leading'` puts the button after the leading items.

A header's revealed title and subtitle on iOS 26 now rise into the bar out of a blur like Mail's title, with the subtitle trailing, and leave the same way backwards. Pick another preset (`blur`, `fade`, `none`) or set offset, blur, scale, delay, spring or easing per line with `titleTransition` on `useHeader` and `useHeroHeader`, or `transition` on `NavigationItemSubtitle`.

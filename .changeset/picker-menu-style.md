`Picker` takes `pickerStyle` on iOS: `wheel`, as before and still the default, or `menu`, a button with the selection, in the accent, that opens the options as a menu. A screen with more than one picker, or a picker in a row, no longer has to give the screen to wheels. Android ignores it: its exposed dropdown is a menu already.
